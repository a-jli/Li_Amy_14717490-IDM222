Not Yet Dawn

Not Yet Dawn is an action-rougelike where you play as a young vampire making her way back home after a night out. Balance life lost to sunlight with life gained by stealing your enemies' blood! Worked as a concept artist/character designer, as well as the lead sprite and key art artist for the game. Iterated on designs to best fit the dev's vision, drew attention-grabbing key art to draw in customers to the game's store page dawn is so cute! put multiple examples of art and iteration here, maybe with design1 and design2 for media. created x amount of images for the game, etc etc.
